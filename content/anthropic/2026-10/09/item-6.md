This page introduces Anthropic's Frontier Red Team, one of six listed research teams. The team says it stress-tests AI systems to understand the full extent of their current capabilities and to anticipate what comes next, providing evidence-based analysis of AI's implications for cybersecurity, national security and autonomous systems.

The page highlights as current work Project Fetch: Phase two, results from a test of whether Claude can help Anthropic employees perform sophisticated robotics tasks. Recent publications listed cover a range of security and autonomy topics: "Launching an opt-in vulnerability-finding service for open-source software" &#40;Oct 8, 2026&#41;; "GLM-5.3 and the spread of advanced cyber capabilities" &#40;Sep 29, 2026&#41;; "Measuring tactical intelligence targeting and conventional weapons capabilities of AI models" &#40;Sep 10, 2026&#41;; "Patterns and problems in emerging multiagent systems" &#40;Aug 13, 2026&#41;; "Discovering cryptographic weaknesses with Claude" &#40;Jul 28, 2026&#41;; "Project Pilot: Can AI control a drone?" &#40;Jul 24, 2026&#41;; "Claude plays robotics" &#40;Jul 9, 2026&#41;; "Project Fetch: Phase two" &#40;Jun 18, 2026&#41;; "Measuring LLMs' impact on N-day exploits" &#40;Jun 8, 2026&#41;; and "Mapping AI-enabled cyber threats: Insights from the LLM ATT&amp;CK Navigator" &#40;Jun 3, 2026&#41;.
