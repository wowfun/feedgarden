Anthropic's Societal Impacts group is a technical research team that studies how AI is used in the real world, working closely with the company's Policy and Safeguards teams. It sits alongside other research teams listed on the page: Alignment, Economics, Interpretability, Societal Impacts, Frontier Red Team, and Sociotechnical alignment.

The team's stated questions include which human values AI models should hold and how they should behave when values conflict or are ambiguous, how AI is used and misused in the wild, and how future uses and risks can be anticipated. To answer them, researchers develop experiments, training methods, and evaluations. The page notes that although the team is technical, it often selects research questions with policy relevance, on the belief that trustworthy research on topics policymakers care about leads to better policy outcomes.

The page lists recent work and publications with dates:

- "What 81,000 people want from AI" &#40;Mar 18, 2026&#41;: an open invitation to Claude.ai users to describe how they use AI, what they hope it makes possible and what they fear; nearly 81,000 people took part, described as the largest and most multilingual qualitative study of its kind.
- "Enabling independent research on how people use Claude" &#40;Aug 26, 2026&#41;: a pilot giving external researchers access to aggregate, real-world Claude usage data, with high-level results and lessons from running it.
- "Claude's values across models and languages" &#40;Jul 13, 2026&#41;: research on how values expressed by Claude vary across the top 20 languages on Claude.ai.
- "Measuring AI agent autonomy in practice" &#40;Feb 18, 2026&#41;: an analysis of millions of human-agent interactions examining how much autonomy people grant agents and how that changes with experience.
- "Introducing Anthropic Interviewer" &#40;Dec 4, 2025&#41;: an interview tool powered by Claude that runs detailed interviews automatically, reporting what 1,250 professionals said about working with AI.

Further listed publications cover topics such as what people want from AI, how people ask Claude for personal guidance, how AI is transforming work at Anthropic, how educators use Claude, how people use Claude for support, advice and companionship, and the Anthropic Economic Index on AI's impact on software development.
