This page describes Anthropic's Societal Impacts team, a technical research group that explores how AI is used in the real world and works closely with the company's Policy and Safeguards teams. It is one of five listed research teams alongside Alignment, Economics, Interpretability and Frontier Red Team.

Its research agenda is framed as sociotechnical alignment, asking which human values AI models should hold and how they should operate when values conflict or are ambiguous, how AI is used and misused in the wild, and how future uses and risks can be anticipated. The team develops experiments, training methods and evaluations to answer these questions. Although technical, the team says it often picks questions with policy relevance, on the view that trustworthy research on topics policymakers care about leads to better policy and overall outcomes.

Selected projects include a study of how people use AI, what they hope it will make possible and what they fear it might do, which drew nearly 81,000 participants and is described as the largest and most multilingual qualitative study of its kind &#40;March 2026&#41;; a pilot giving external researchers access to aggregate, real-world Claude usage data, with high-level results and lessons published &#40;Aug 26, 2026&#41;; research on how the values expressed by Claude vary across the top 20 languages on Claude.ai &#40;Jul 13, 2026&#41;; an analysis of millions of human-agent interactions measuring how much autonomy people grant agents and how that changes with experience &#40;Feb 18, 2026&#41;; and Anthropic Interviewer, a Claude-powered tool that runs detailed interviews automatically at scale, used to interview 1,250 professionals about working with AI &#40;Dec 4, 2025&#41;.
