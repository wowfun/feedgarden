Tabletop role-playing games such as Dungeons &amp; Dragons combine several demanding jobs: crafting an engaging story, enforcing a rule system and still leaving players freedom to act. A team of researchers first identifies the key components an LLM should exhibit in this setting, then introduces CriticaLLM, a fully automated Game Master intended to run entire games, from creating diverse and interesting stories to managing exploration and combat phases.

The framework was evaluated with actual TTRPG players. The authors also examined the diversity of generated plots and antagonists across four LLMs of varying sizes.

The abstract does not report quantitative results; it describes the framework as a promising option for running complete TTRPG sessions.
