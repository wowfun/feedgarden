OntoAct is a hypothesis-based neuro-symbolic framework that uses ontological abstraction to turn individual experiences into axioms over entity types, relations and conditions, so that the underlying patterns can be reused in new situations. A large language model proposes hypotheses and actions, while a symbolic verifier checks their supporting conditions against current observations. Verification exposes missing evidence, and new observations and intervention outcomes update the assessments of hypotheses and guide subsequent actions.

The authors also introduce Tacit-FLE, a benchmark built on the Factorio Learning Environment for diagnosing and repairing hidden faults in existing factories using inherited operational records. Its synthetic maintenance corpus distributes evidence across records, and an executed repair may leave a fault unresolved, forcing agents to reassess their diagnoses through interaction. Across Tacit-FLE, Mars and ALFWorld, OntoAct reports leading task performance while using interactions and inference efficiently.
