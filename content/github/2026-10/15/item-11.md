f/prompts.chat is the project formerly known as Awesome ChatGPT Prompts. It is a place to share, discover and collect prompts contributed by a community of users.

The project is described as free and open source. It can be self-hosted so that an organization keeps the prompts it uses internal, with the description emphasizing complete privacy as the reason to run it locally rather than through a public service.
