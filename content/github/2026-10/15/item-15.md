freecodecamp/freeCodeCamp is described as the open-source codebase and curriculum behind freeCodeCamp.org. The stated offering is free learning in math, programming and computer science.

In other words, the repository covers both the software that runs the platform and the curriculum material itself, and the described purpose is to let people study those subjects at no cost.
