iptv-org/iptv is a collection of publicly available IPTV channels from around the world. The project gathers into one repository links to internet television streams that are openly available, organized as a shared list rather than a single broadcaster's service.

Its scope is global, and the value comes from aggregation: instead of searching for individual streams, users can draw on a maintained collection covering channels in many countries and languages. This kind of list is typically used with IPTV players and other software capable of reading channel playlists.

The repository is open source, so the list and the surrounding tooling can be checked, forked or self-hosted. The supplied description does not state how many channels are included, how often the list is updated, or whether streams are checked for availability; it also makes no claim about the legality or licensing status of individual streams in any particular jurisdiction. Users should therefore treat availability and rights questions as matters to verify on their own.

In short, it is a browsing and reference resource for globally distributed free-to-air internet television channels, not a content provider itself.
