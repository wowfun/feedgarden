awesome-selfhosted/awesome-selfhosted is described as a list of Free Software network services and web applications that users can host on their own servers. The entry covers both network services and web applications, and the defining requirement stated is that the software be free &#40;libre&#41; and self-hostable.

The source gives no number of listed projects, no categorisation scheme and no licence details for individual entries. Its practical use is as a discovery index for people who want to run services on their own infrastructure instead of relying on third-party hosting, but nothing in the material supports claims about the security, reliability or maintenance status of the listed projects.
