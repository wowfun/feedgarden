## HN discussion

Based on 7 selected comments and replies.

The question asked whether people subscribe to hosted LLMs or run open-source models locally, and why. Answers split several ways.

Several commenters said economics decide it. One said running a local model does not pay off for them but likes the privacy and safety appeal, using Claude plus Apple's on-device models for small tasks like parsing recipes. Another subscribes only when a project needs it and skips local models because their 16GB+32GB RAM machine is not worth running anything on once a dev environment and browser are accounted for, citing a &#36;10/month coding option as a no-brainer. A project manager in climate said ease of use and budget drove a subscription; they lack either the budget for a capable machine or the knowledge to get one for about &#36;20/month.

Others reported hybrid or local-first setups. One uses Claude/Codex subscriptions for most coding but experiments with ollama models and trains small CPU-based local classifiers. Another runs open models exclusively on their own hardware even though their employer demands "the frontier" and offers unlimited spend and quota — partly for repetitive work, partly to test how delivering without tracked token usage is received.

One commenter declined both options: "I am neither. I am an actual human person," prompting a joking reply questioning that.
