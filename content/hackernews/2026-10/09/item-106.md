## Article

OldRoll Web is a browser-based retro photo editor submitted to Show HN. Per its description, it runs on desktop and mobile with no installation and is offered free online. Users pick from camera-inspired presets such as Digicam, Film, Y2K, 90s, VHS, Disposable Camera, Dreamy and an "H35" look, then adjust exposure, contrast, warmth, saturation and lens blur, crop the image and compare the edit against the original. The editor also layers film frames, flashes, grain, light leaks and stickers over a base look, and can assemble one or several photos into a collage with a solid, gradient or textured background.

The copy positions the tool for content creators, photography enthusiasts, subculture and Y2K creators, online sellers, musicians making cover art and anime or cosplay creators. It claims the OldRoll camera experience has been used for "2B+ photos and videos" and lists short testimonials from users dated 2024 through 2026, praising the vintage rendering, instant-print looks and lifetime access purchased during a sale, while some note that advertisements can be irritating and wish for a desktop edition or video capture in the H35 style.

The submission text is largely marketing material, so claims such as the usage figure and user sentiment come from the vendor and are not independently verified here.

## HN discussion

Based on 3 selected comments and replies.

Commenters responded positively. AnthonyR said they had seen a paid product advertising similar effects on Instagram and questioned why camera filters should cost money, and suggested the trend of "retro-ifying" modern camera photos may reflect phone cameras becoming so good that images lose character. The submitter, awa11, replied thanking them for trying the product and inviting suggestions, describing the project's aim as providing high-quality camera filter assets to every user. A third commenter, pony17620, joked that the retro experience is so authentic it even reproduced waiting for film to develop, quoting an on-screen message that only the last successful result is shown because new settings have not been generated.
