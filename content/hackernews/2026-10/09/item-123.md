## HN discussion

Based on 10 selected comments and replies.

Several commenters argue that fake opinion pieces and sock-puppet accounts are old tactics, and that what is new is a highly centralized tool that also logs usage and reports to authorities and news outlets. One reply pushes back, saying the more structurally relevant novelty is the sharp drop in the cost of running such a campaign, and that discussion of cost reduction is being underweighted.

A second thread questions how placement works: one commenter asks whether freelancers are paid to write polished opinion pieces for obscure outlets that then pass them to larger sites, and a reply says PR departments have planted stories for a long time, needing only a few willing writers. Others argue that given self-hosting options, many governments likely do this at some scale, and that the deeper problem is weak verification. A related concern is AI outputs being poisoned by propaganda, citing reporting on millions of Russian propaganda articles incorporated into Western AI systems in 2024, with an expressed hope that training filters have improved. Separately, one commenter praised the article for focusing on the operators rather than treating ChatGPT as the actor, urging the same wording elsewhere. These are commenter views, not reported facts.
