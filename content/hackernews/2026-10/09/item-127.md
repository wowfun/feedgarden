## Article

This is the text of an EU promotional landing page encouraging researchers to pursue careers in Europe. It frames the European Union as "a world-leading centre for research and innovation with freedom of science" offering "stable and predictable funding opportunities" and cutting-edge facilities, and argues that EU funding turns "laboratory discovery" into "market-ready innovation" at every stage.

The page highlights specific schemes. The European Commission has announced a boost of more than €210 million to the Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions &#40;MSCA&#41;, described as creating opportunities for more than 700 additional researchers. It cites Horizon Europe as "the world's largest research programme at €93.5 billion," supporting all scientific domains from fundamental discovery to applied innovation, and says it avoids short funding cycles or predetermined research outcomes. Doctoral Networks are said to fund PhD positions with a full employment contract, monthly living and mobility allowances, cross-sector secondments and access to a pan-European research community; individual PhD positions from earlier successful calls are advertised on EURAXESS.

A separate call period is described as letting successful applicants select, recruit and train post-doctoral researchers with "excellent working conditions," with the stated aim of converting "brain drain" into "brain gain"; those individual positions are advertised on EURAXESS from September 2026. Multiple funding schemes are said to open throughout the year.

The page also points to application support through EU Delegations' information sessions on funding, eligibility and application processes, and personalised help from EURAXESS. Practical guidance covers coming to Europe &#40;visas, entry conditions, residence permits, short-term stays, hosting&#41;, working in Europe &#40;work permits, taxation, salaries, diploma recognition, intellectual property rights, career development&#41; and living in Europe &#40;accommodation, health insurance, banking, childcare, languages&#41;, citing welfare systems and diversity commitments. The page states it was last updated on 5 October 2026. Its claims are promotional and not independently verified here.
