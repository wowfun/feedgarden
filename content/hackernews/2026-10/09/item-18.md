## Article

A satirical developer-tooling page, formatted as a GitHub-style project README under the name GitHub.meme, presents a fictional task board called Flowboard from quiet-labs. It bills itself as a simple, fast, and delightfully boring task board for developers, written in TypeScript, powered by coffee and maintained by humans.

Getting started is described in four commands: clone the Flowboard repository, cd flowboard, npm install, and npm run dev, promising setup in under a minute, probably. The badges list a passing build, npm v2.4.1, an MIT license, and a note that PRs are welcome.

The humor targets common open-source marketing and maintenance habits: a Why Flowboard section offers the slogan your workflow, your rules, drag, drop, ship, and no magic, just 47 dependencies and a dream, while a community section promises that someone will review your PR eventually. A note says the project is experimenting with an AI coding assistant with the aside what could go wrong. The contributing section tells readers to fork it, make a branch, open a pull request, and wait for a maintainer to return from their 2019 sabbatical. The page is a joke site rather than a real project announcement.
