## HN discussion

Based on 5 selected comments and replies.

The sampled comments are largely dismissive of Thiel's lectures on the Antichrist. One commenter argues there is a very short walk from serious concern about religious prophecy to people asking why anyone takes the speaker seriously, and answers that the reason is large amounts of money.

Another turns the framing around: why wouldn't the Antichrist be the one spreading warnings that someone or something else is the Antichrist, since misdirection prevents suspicion, adding, half-jokingly, that he could charge one hundred dollars a head to peddle it. A reply quotes coverage of the lectures, noting Thiel drew on political theory and pop culture, ranging from Francis Bacon to Ozymandias in Watchmen and the manga One Piece, all of whom he believes are or include candidates for the Antichrist, and that he invokes Nazi Party member Carl Schmitt, whose Katechon ideas he counts as an influence and slightly critiques. That commenter imagines Vatican readers reacting angrily. Another asks whether this is the Peter Thiel who was a friend of a disgraced financier, and questions why anyone should care what he thinks beyond knowing what an adversary is doing. One comment supplies an archive link.
