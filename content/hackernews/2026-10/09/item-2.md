## HN discussion

Based on 9 selected comments and replies.

Commenters compared StepFun's new model against alternatives and prior Step releases. syntaxing said Step models were, in their view, the first local models that worked well on 128GB of shared memory, but was disappointed to learn Step 5 is a 600B-A27B model that cannot run on 228GB. girvo called Step 3.5 and 3.7 Flash their favourite local models earlier in the year, said Step 5 is far too large for their DGX Spark-like machine, and hoped it would be faster than GLM given promising benchmarks; alfiedotwtf replied that Step 3.5 was the first model that convinced them LLMs were not a fad.

On capability, robertlane0 cited Artificial Analysis as saying it is smarter and slightly cheaper than Gemini 3.8 Flash, their baseline for cheap and sufficiently smart, and planned to try it on OpenCode for a week without committing to switching from Muse Spark 1.3. Roark66 argued benchmark results should be mandatory at the top of any new model release page. theturtletalks defended the interest, noting StepFun's Step 3.5 was near state of the art before being overtaken, and that open models push the frontier even for users of closed models. Commenters also speculated about identity: lykr0n wondered whether this was the "Space Bunny Alpha" model, and minimaxir said after quick tests it likely was not, adding that Space Bunny Alpha showed hints of being a MiniMax variant.
