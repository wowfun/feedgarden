## HN discussion

Based on 7 selected comments and replies.

Commenters largely read the video as a marker of where generative media is heading. One notes the song was actually made with Suno v6 rather than Opus, calling Suno impressive but "one huge copyright violation" &#40;ofrzeta&#41;. Reflecting on a linked alternative video, a commenter says that although they dislike the genre, the output "sounds better than most other music made by real pop artists," concluding LLMs are "fantastic at imitating mediocre art" &#40;glimshe&#41;. Others predict personalized entertainment: musical and visual genres tailored to individuals, groups and communities, with human artists likely unhappy even as some build further on these tools &#40;anilgulecha&#41;. A dissenting view expects a split market, in which cheap, accessible personalized work is consumed regardless of quality while verifiably unique, high-quality goods command soaring prices &#40;Charly&#95;HW&#41;. Another commenter compares the shift to feeling like a typist at an old Fortune 500 job, calling it cool but unsettling &#40;karim79&#41;.
