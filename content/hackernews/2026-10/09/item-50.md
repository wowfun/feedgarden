## HN discussion

Based on 6 selected comments and replies.

Commenters disagree about why this episode escalated. Centigonal observes that although OpenAI looks superficially very similar to Anthropic, it seems to consistently generate more drama, and calls that difference significant without settling its cause. solyin replies that the difference is cultural, framing the choice as one between AI run by "sociopaths" and AI run by "Europeans with a strict moral code." potsandpans is unsympathetic to departing safety researchers who describe OpenAI as much more than a job: people who dedicate their lives to a corporation should not be surprised when the profit motive wins, and the generous benefit of the doubt the public gives them contrasts with how climate researchers were treated. impossiblefork counters that OpenAI's nonprofit foundation still holds some power, so staff could reasonably expect the firm to care, and criticizes its alignment with the US Department of Defense as inconsistent with a pledge to benefit all of humanity.
