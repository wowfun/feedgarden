## HN discussion

Based on 7 selected comments and replies.

Commenters split on whether the &#36;1049 Steam Machine is good value. One argues it is a bargain if you value your time and want Linux: it plays out of the box, is well maintained, and unlike a console can be switched to a full desktop. Another calls it overpriced for the hardware, pointing to a roughly &#36;1,100 prebuilt with an Intel Core Ultra 5 225F, RTX 5060, 32GB RAM and 1TB SSD, and notes anti-cheat is game-specific: EAC works on Linux when the developer enables a per-game flag, so buyers should check the titles they play. A third suggests GeForce Now Ultimate at &#36;20 a month instead, which would take about 52 months, or over four years, to match the purchase price, and says the money is better spent on a self-built machine or a streaming subscription. On the positive side, one commenter calls reliable wake-from-sleep the Steam Machine's killer feature, crediting Valve with solving it at the hardware level, and another replies that Microsoft's removal of S3 sleep support is what made this hard on ordinary HTPCs. A further commenter argues the compact size is the only real point of the device since Bazzite can be installed elsewhere, and that the appeal is novelty that fades.
