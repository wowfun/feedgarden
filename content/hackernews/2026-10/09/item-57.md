## HN discussion

Based on 5 selected comments and replies.

Commenters discussed mitigation and motive, without firm attribution. One noted that Cloudflare already mirrors Arch and Debian and suggested it could help with Ubuntu too, and that Facebook runs a large distro mirror; they also observed that the attack targets the Canonical security API and ISOs rather than the package archives, though those might be mirrored as well. Others wondered what anyone gains from DDoS-ing a project like this and what the motive could be. One reply suggested it is probably ransom — a group demanding money or taking you offline for a while — or possibly someone upset about Ubuntu's direction trying to force certain actions, adding that people can be petty and greedy. A short speculative comment guessing at national origin went unsubstantiated; another replied with a sarcastic reference to Windows updates and WSL. No one in the sample provided evidence of who is behind the attack.
