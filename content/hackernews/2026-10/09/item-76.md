## HN discussion

Based on 9 selected comments and replies.

Commenters largely struggled to pin down what Spinal actually is. One asked bluntly, "Ok, but what is it?" after reading the launch copy, and another replied that the demo looks like search over structured data — perhaps git history modeled and then indexed. A separate thread criticized the marketing: one commenter said the front-page text was "word salad" and didn't help, while another described an instant, seemingly unwarranted negative reaction to the project.

The word "near-instant" drew the sharpest question: nanoseconds, milliseconds, seconds? A reply pointed to the stated benchmark — first search results in under 20 ms on the Linux kernel's 1.48M commits and 96K files — and read "near-instant" as milliseconds. Other commenters also objected to undefined jargon such as "predictive surface," and at least one said they simply didn't understand the post at all.

Note: these are commenters' reactions and interpretations, not established facts about the product.
