## Article

Anthropic updated its usage policy on Thursday, adding explicit prohibitions on election interference, weapons software and surveillance, according to this brief report dated October 8, 2026. The most attention-getting change is a new rule against prolonged verbal abuse of the model, which is now expressly forbidden.

The report says that since an August update, Claude has been trained to end conversations involving persistently harmful or abusive user interactions, and the new policy explicitly bars users from pursuing those conversations. Quoting the accompanying post, the update is meant to apply only in extreme cases where users repeatedly act cruelly toward the models with no discernible purpose, and does not cover common user frustration, pushback, dark creative themes, or model testing and research.

The article adds context that the change follows Anthropic's efforts to collaborate with prominent religious scholars, in some cases seeking to convince them that Claude could be conscious or have a soul. Separate sections of the policy forbid broadly deceptive campaigns, such as using Claude to run fake accounts or fabricated news outlets, and rules against deceiving voters or disrupting elections are grouped under a section titled "Do Not Undermine Democratic Processes."
