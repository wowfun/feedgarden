## HN discussion

Based on 5 selected comments and replies.

Commenters split over the plan to livestream the execution. One called the idea inhuman, asking whether a public execution is really necessary. A reply agreed the initial reaction was distasteful, describing the prospect as feeding a bloodlust nobody wants to grow, but then argued the actual broadcast may be far more limited: public access could be delayed and edited or obscured, perhaps as a slideshow of stills, with only personnel on the base watching live, since live video from inside military bases is not normally released. That commenter said time would tell. Another asked bluntly whether this amounts to government-funded snuff films, and a further commenter called it shameful. One commenter simply linked an earlier, related submission.
