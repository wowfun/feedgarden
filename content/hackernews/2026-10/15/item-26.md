## HN discussion

Based on 10 selected comments and replies.

A recurring concern is data residency and retention. One commenter notes the vendor mentions zero data retention &#40;ZDR&#41; for enterprise accounts only in its support material, calls that a hard showstopper, and says the team has been slow to answer; they benchmarked self-hosted alternatives and still found nothing matching Jev's cost/performance. A reply agrees they would adopt immediately if ZDR were offered together with optional EU residency, especially with the new image capability, and points to a Digital Ocean offering as a possible route.

On local alternatives, one developer says they added image support to their own OpenDecision project days ago, with video planned next; a reply asks about hardware requirements given it runs locally.

Reaction to the launch post is mixed. One user says they like the tone and are already subscribed, quoting the deliberate context-length limit and the note that users are no longer treated like children. Another user welcomes a model with image bounding-box support and wishes for segmentation, saying a Gemini Flash-Lite model is mandatory in their workflow for semantic segmentation; a reply asks why not SAM or Moondream and where it outperforms. One user mentions building a GUI, Decision Studio, for experimenting with Jev recipes, tasks, criteria and probability distributions, requiring an OpenRouter API key, and another appreciates the explicit warning not to trust AI benchmarks, arguing benchmarks can be gamed and should be validated against one's own use case.
