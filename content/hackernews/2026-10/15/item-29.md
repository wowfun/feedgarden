## HN discussion

Based on 8 selected comments and replies.

Commenters treated the 46% quarter-over-quarter increase as a deceleration rather than a win. pvab3 said the quarter was up 46% from one that had itself grown 140%, concluding that while revenue is rising this is "not good news for Anthropic" because it is not the exponential curve that was expected. In reply, no-name-here questioned whether Anthropic ever promised exponential growth, and noted a reported 2028 target of &#36;195B would require roughly 15% growth per period.

Others turned to costs and financing. claaams asked whether the revenue even covers interest on the company's loans, and nly predicted the bonds would be converted to equity and sold to retail investors at IPO. Grimblewald said the size of the increase made him "shudder" at how much expenses must have grown.

jacquesm argued the biggest risk is the committed expenditure level: if Anthropic is not yet profitable at that point, or cannot raise more money, it could be in serious trouble. Separately, perarneng said they were considering moving to Gemini, reasoning that Google's existing customer deals give it an advantage.
