## Article

The maintainer of a long-running repository of terminal color schemes has launched a companion web application, terminalthemes.com, turning a project he had said he would "someday" convert into a site into a working product after roughly 15 years.

The app catalogs more than 800 themes and offers downloads in close to 40 formats, covering iTerm, Ghostty, Windows Terminal and others. Browsing and downloading require no account and are free; registering, also free, adds the ability to vote, bookmark, submit themes and claim authorship of themes previously contributed to the GitHub repository. Find My Theme and other discovery tools are included, alongside live terminal and code previews intended to give a more realistic view than the ANSI screenshots the repository used, which remain available. Accessibility features were also a focus. Themes added on the site sync back to the GitHub repository and vice versa, and contributors to the original repo are invited to register, locate their themes and use a Claim Authorship button.

The author credits the community that contributed themes, ports to other terminals and format conversion tools over the years for the collection's breadth.

## HN discussion

Based on 10 selected comments and replies.

The author noted that themes added on the site sync back to the GitHub repository and the other way around.

nmstoker asked whether light/dark classification is automatic and suggested using color-distance heuristics to browse themes similar to one you nearly like. The author replied that classification is automatic but can be overridden in admin tools, acknowledged that the Similar Themes feature predates later work on accessibility, tags and color profiles and may need revisiting, and pointed to the Find My Theme questionnaire.

blackfawn reported that the "remember" function did not work correctly in guest mode: it appeared functional when defaulted to iTerm, but became unclickable after deselecting that and choosing other terminals actually used. The author confirmed that the setting is available to registered users in preferences, said stickiness was likely not implemented for guest mode and was probably overlooked, and thanked the reporter.

manu29d described using the repository and its themes on every new machine for at least eight to ten years and returning to it despite trying alternatives. d0utone asked for larger, more visible theme previews in the main feed so themes can be scrolled on a phone without opening each one; the author said mobile work is planned but not yet a focus.
