## HN discussion

Based on 10 selected comments and replies.

Commenters reacted skeptically rather than sympathetically. One thread &#40;50101109&#41; criticizes MacRumors for admitting it knew the device existed but chose not to publicize its release, arguing that letting such a product slip to market makes the outlet complicit and that "the standards you walk past are the standards you accept."

Others question the product itself: one commenter &#40;50099980&#41; says they do not follow what unique technology is actually protected, and describes simply drilling through the offending PCB element to silence a beeping device, noting AirTags are cheap enough to practice on. A reply &#40;50100014&#41; argues the company's magnetic AirTag mount is arguably just as dangerous, since it can be attached under a car in a second without anyone inside hearing it; another &#40;50104528&#41; counters that they usually get the "AirTag is moving with you" notification quickly, suggesting only the silencing tech would prevent it.

Practical interest also appears: a commenter &#40;50100342&#41; asks what the best current option is for tracking bike thieves across their six bikes. Separately, a commenter &#40;50099704&#41; notes the "read more" link points to the same page, calling the absence of any further content suspicious.
