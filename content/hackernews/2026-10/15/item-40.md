## HN discussion

Based on 10 selected comments and replies.

Commenters used the release as a prompt for a broader distribution debate. ilaksh says Ubuntu is no longer fashionable and points to Fedora or Arch setups with slick Wayland-era interfaces as where interest has moved. bayindirh offers a menu: Debian Stable or Rocky Linux for servers, Debian Stable for people willing to put in work who do not need bleeding-edge software, Debian Testing for hands-on desktops in secure environments that serve nothing, Fedora for those who want something that just works, and Arch, Gentoo or Linux From Scratch for anyone going deeper — noting Debian needs work not because it fails out of the box but because it does no package customisation. flakiness stays with Ubuntu as boring and occasionally breaking, yet "at least it's not hostile"; shrx rejects that framing, calling the silent forced replacement of deb packages with snaps during regular apt updates hostile. throw0101c notes this release still provides GNU coreutils alongside the Rust-based uutils. erelong left over dropped X11 support, and amelius asks only whether Snap's main usability problems have finally been fixed or at least acknowledged.
