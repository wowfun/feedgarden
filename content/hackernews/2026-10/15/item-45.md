## HN discussion

Based on 6 selected comments and replies.

Commenters are enthusiastic about the experiment and its departure from current desktop design. One says the era around twenty years ago, citing Enlightenment, was more fun than today's "bland monotonic design", and argues that while plain modern looks should remain an option, more variety is needed. Several call the project lovely or fantastic and hope more such experiments appear, with one wishing that society would "return to chasing beauty" again. One commenter finds it amusing but says it would feel like working inside a game such as Papers Please; another compares it to Microsoft Bob and suggests adding a talking animal assistant, optionally wired to an LLM. A short comment simply asks for skeuomorphic design to be taken further. The sample is positive overall, with little technical detail about how the desktop works.
