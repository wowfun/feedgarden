## HN discussion

Based on 9 selected comments and replies.

Commenters focus less on the alleged incidents than on how the petition is being framed. One points out that the widely repeated figure of 1,000 is the size of the Robotaxi fleet, not a count of incidents, and argues the petition alleges three, linking the NHTSA document. Others joke that the driving amounts to re-enacting existing training data, or to running an experiment meant to create new training data.

An owner reports that their FSD 14.3 car does not respect school zones and says they expect a hard cutoff at the posted speed limit there regardless of which driving mode is active; a reply wishes the system would at least behave as gently as its most cautious mode below 30 mph.

A separate thread notes that small towns have at times funded themselves through speed traps, and imagines a sheriff generating fines by ticketing Tesla robotaxis. No comment votes are supplied; order reflects ranking only.
