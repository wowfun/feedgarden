## HN discussion

Based on 10 selected comments and replies.

Commenters broadly welcomed the goal but pressed on mechanics and credibility. Sohcahtoa82 argued that offline modes make no sense for some online games such as MMORPGs, so studios should release server binaries and let players choose a server; dawnerd replied that the server component or documentation of how it works would suffice, while accepting that releasing a full backend stack may be unreasonable. Thegn raised a loophole: a company could ship a crippling final patch and then release only that version. JumpCrisscross questioned why the bill text was not published and wanted a revenue floor so indie developers are not sued over improperly wound-down hobby servers; Tangurena2 noted the bill has not yet been introduced. Animats and others called the announcement a likely PR stunt without co-sponsors.
