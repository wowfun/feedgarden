## HN discussion

Based on 10 selected comments and replies.

With little description of the site itself, commenters debated what a developer social network should be. ineptech imagined people separately building bespoke apps, with agents trading magic links and negotiating key exchanges; flexagoon replied that this largely amounts to hosting a website, possibly with an agent given server access, and pointed to tilde.town. After a commenter posted sitemaps of users and commits, notpsychopath argued that the GitHub usernames and commit posts are already public and no security concern. SwellJoe praised Mastodon as home to people motivated by love of the craft, while Zambyte said a local agent revived a Pleroma instance that had been down for about two years without backups, only to find a feed dominated by LLM criticism and thin on technical content.
