## HN discussion

Based on 10 selected comments and replies.

Commenters debate why a marketplace is enforcing spectrum rules. One asks why eBay is 'acting as the FCC,' noting that the equipment is legal to own and to transmit with the proper license, which is not required to buy it, and that other risky items such as lasers remain listed. A reply attributes such policies to large legal departments minimizing regulatory and reputational risk, grouping radio gear with knives, fireworks and laboratory chemicals, and says payment processors impose similar limits. Others defend the restriction's purpose: keeping modified Baofengs off frequencies reserved for licensed services or emergency responders. A reply recounts that unlocked handhelds were once mistaken for ordinary walkie-talkies, that locked-down models now require deliberate unlocking, and that FRS/GMRS sets are an easier legal option. One commenter notes listings must carry an FCC ID, so most consumer goods remain available; another worries Meshtastic devices could be caught.
