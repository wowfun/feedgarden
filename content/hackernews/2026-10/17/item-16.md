## HN discussion

Based on 10 selected comments and replies.

Commenters focus less on the rating itself than on how it was produced. One notes that Anthropic's submission to KBRA omitted free cash flow and EBITDA, forcing analysts to estimate them and KBRA to use a 'risk score' instead of full financial models; using a smaller agency adds to the doubt. A reply says a trillion-dollar company that supplies too little to its rating agency looks bad whether it withheld the data or never had it. Others see echoes of past credit cycles: one asks what happened last time major agencies gave sketchy debt generous ratings to avoid alienating customers, and a reply answers that the subprime analogy is weak because Anthropic's business is widely understood. A skeptic calls the story not particularly newsworthy because Kroll/KBRA is not a major agency used by bond buyers; another replies that choosing an outlier agency is itself the signal.
