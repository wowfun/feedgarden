## HN discussion

Based on 4 selected comments and replies.

Commenters focused on the numbers. One compared the tracker's probabilities with other forecasters: it put Democrats' House chances at 0.80 versus 0.96 for The Economist and 0.99 for FiftyPlusOne, while its 0.63 Senate estimate sat nearer their 0.58 and 0.73. That commenter quoted the site's methodology — a blend of recent polls and Polymarket/Kalshi odds, with polls weighted more heavily as surveys accumulate, and chamber-control odds drawn from 10,000 simulations that account for shared national and state-level shifts — and noted the site calls these experimental estimates, not guarantees. Another said the approach closely resembles electionbettingodds.com, which has combined such data for years.
