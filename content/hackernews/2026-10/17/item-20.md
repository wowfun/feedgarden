## HN discussion

Based on 10 selected comments and replies.

Commenters debated the legal risk and the practical value of the project. One urged the authors to host it outside GitHub, arguing that Sony could take the page down at any time; others focused on what the hack enables. One said the appeal is turning a locked-down appliance into a general-purpose computer, while a reply countered that the console is under-specced for its price. A detailed comment reported running Qwen 3.8 27B at a 2-bit quant and called that adequate for long-running, low-stakes token streams, estimating about 70 cents a day in power for roughly 2 million output tokens, or about &#36;3.50 worth of usage. Another noted that BC250 boards built around cut-down PS5 silicon exist, but a reply said they are hard to find at &#36;150–&#36;200 and that an RTX 3060 is a less troublesome alternative.
