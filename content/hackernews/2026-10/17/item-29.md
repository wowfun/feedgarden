## HN discussion

Based on 4 selected comments and replies.

Commenters focused on provenance and on how much the claim can be trusted. One pointed to a separate, larger HN thread on the same story, titled "Claude reveals ECDSA curve origins," saying the submitter's link had been added to that thread's top text, so much of the discussion lives there.

Reacting to the post's account that the seeds were instead generated with the POSIX-standard drand48 random number generator, one commenter remarked that memory can play tricks on everyone — or that someone may have lied to obscure the origin. A reply argued that disclosing the origin would have been beneficial, and that lying for obfuscation makes no sense given what is now known.

Another commenter used the thread to solicit other seed-based swag ideas, citing an obfuscated, self-evaluating bash script from Akamai that was supplied to consumers through retail stores.
