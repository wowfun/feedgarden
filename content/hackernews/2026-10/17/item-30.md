## HN discussion

Based on 10 selected comments and replies.

Commenters were sharply divided over the value of the code-review setup. avinoth compared it to Google infrastructure write-ups that smaller teams copy at their peril, arguing Anthropic can absorb the token cost while other shops still need code comprehension; Rapzid replied that he doubted the scale was necessary for a conceptually simple CLI. vb-8448 said no human can stay on par with 30,000 pull requests a day and asked about churn and cost per line, suspecting repeated rewrites. koolba called it insane to let an LLM write documentation that nobody reads. jonahx argued that retrieving a summary is not the same as holding a causal model of the system, warning that nobody may be able to diagnose a critical bug. TNWin dismissed the post as out of touch and hype-driven; other replies questioned whether its prose was AI-written.
