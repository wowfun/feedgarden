## HN discussion

Based on 5 selected comments and replies.

Commenters focus on the customer experience rather than the retention claim. One says the Taco Bell drive-thru now opens with an AI prompt, but that they and everyone they have seen there wait for a human to answer, and they will not order from a robot: "Give me a kiosk or give me a human." Another finds that fair and points to the article's statement that the system also helps food-delivery orders as the more plausible use case. A third jokes that customers already pretend Taco Bell's food is food, so pretending to talk to a human should be easy; a reply asks in what sense Taco Bell is not food. No commenter directly addresses the employee-retention numbers.
