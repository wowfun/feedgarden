## HN discussion

Based on 10 selected comments and replies.

Commenters with early access reported sharply different results for FLUX 3 Image Klein. One said Reddit users find it weak at generating people, speculating that near-NSFW material was stripped from training; another, testing locally, called text-to-image very poor, saying the stock ComfyUI template produced SD 1.5-level melted faces and body horror, though Flux 2 Klein 9b also had anatomical problems. A third praised editing as excellent but was disappointed in fine text detail and said community discussion asks "why Flux?" after Moss Image appeared stronger for text-to-image; a reply noted MOSS is Apache 2.0 licensed while many open-weight models carry unusual restrictions. On price, one commenter cited &#36;0.009 per 1K images as competitive against Krea 3 at &#36;0.06 per image, while another replied that quality does not compete. Others marvelled that a sub-10GB model holds world knowledge.
