## HN discussion

Based on 8 selected comments and replies.

Commenters focused on what GNOME OS actually is and how it is assembled. One noted that it does not appear to be built on Debian, Arch or RHEL and treats Flatpak as its dependency mechanism, prompting a plan to try it in a VM. A reply corrected that Flatpak is more an application manager, comparable to winget, than a traditional package manager, and said the components are built with BuildStream; a possible move to mkosi for image building had not happened when they last looked.

Others complained that the post and GNOME's site explain the preview release but not the OS itself. One reported the ISO failing to boot in QEMU/KVM with SeaBIOS messages about being unable to read the CD-ROM, ending in no bootable device. A skeptical thread questioned GNOME's long-standing support culture, while a reply said GNOME's shift from highly customizable layouts toward a more Mac-like look had reduced its appeal.
