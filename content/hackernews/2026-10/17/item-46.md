## HN discussion

Based on 4 selected comments and replies.

Commenters greeted the report with little surprise. One wrote sarcastically that this was "a shocking and totally unpredictable result." Another commenter cited claims that footage from Meta smart glasses had been sent to contract workers in Kenya for data labeling used to train AI models, including disturbing clips and intimate bathroom recordings made by users who likely did not know third parties would view them; the commenter concluded that Meta cannot resist spying on its users. A further reply asked who would buy such devices, calling them creeps. The longest comment addressed trust: al&#95;borland said bugs happen, but when a product's acceptance depends on public trust and the public already distrusts the company, such failures cannot happen. He said each incident pushes acceptance further away, doubted Zuckerberg could rebuild the trust he needs, and referenced a Joanna Stern interview about WhatsApp encryption and differing platform assumptions, questioning how Meta's business model could be reconciled with that trust.
