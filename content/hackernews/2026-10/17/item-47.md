## Article

Fakeipedia is a Show HN project that automatically builds a wiki for an imagined world: it takes a world premise as input and generates linked encyclopedia articles that readers can explore by following red links. The author says the idea came from experimenting with worldbuilding in Gemini, but he waited to release it until a model, Argon, was creative enough to produce articles worth reading, and he was surprised by how engaging some of the output is.

Generating a new article costs &#36;0.90, which the author says covers AI and Stripe fees and roughly breaks even. Users can steer generation in a choose-your-own-adventure style while creating an article. All generated material is free and public to view, and the author invites suggestions for new worlds and features.
