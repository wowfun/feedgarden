## HN discussion

Based on 5 selected comments and replies.

Commenters debated accountability more than the intrusion itself. osquar argued that only a fine against Burger King would deter such breaches; otherwise the conversation drifts toward the attacker instead of the company's security. Macha said the case shows why Burger King should not have offered an app with accounts linking all that customer information, and why people should avoid such apps, while acknowledging that companies want registered users. fuzzfactor asked why most customers would have a record with a fast food chain at all. tiew9Vii replied that large chains effectively force or at least strongly encourage participation, and argued that Hacker News readers who build "data driven" systems are complicit, citing a recent McDonald's visit.
