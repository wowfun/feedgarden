## HN discussion

Based on 2 selected comments and replies.

Commenters push back on the idea that AI spells doom for mathematics. One argues that individual mathematicians' careers may well be disrupted — researchers who spent years on a problem may have to pursue a new direction — but the field itself has never been healthier, and what will really change is the definition of a "hard problem." They also reject Terence Tao's framing that mathematics is "contaminated" by knowing some problems are likely already solved, asking whether a few more Ramanujans and their manuscripts would likewise contaminate the field. A reply agrees, suggesting the pessimism comes from people who doubted their own aptitude or felt impostor syndrome and now have something to blame, while figures such as Tao, Torvalds, Carmack and 3Blue1Brown's author seem to accept or even welcome AI.
