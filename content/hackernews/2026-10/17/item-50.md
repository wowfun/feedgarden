## HN discussion

Based on 10 selected comments and replies.

Commenters used the Butlerian Jihad as a lens on AI risk. cautiouscat argued that Dune differs from Terminator-style stories because the humans who run the thinking machines, not the machines alone, bring humanity close to ruin, and the Jihad fought those technocrats as much as the machines; AlotOfReading disputed this, saying the technocrat cyborgs had already been enslaved by a machine leader by the inciting incident. antihipocrat cited a passage on the page invoking Heidegger's thesis that using technology trains people to think like machines, and wondered whether LLMs' probabilistic outputs might instead help human thought; atleastoptimal replied that LLMs' probability range is narrow and tuned to be predictable enough for a consumer product, so their ideas repeat. spoaceman7777 said banning AI is a necessary plot device because grand fiction needs conflict, and warned that fiction may lull modern audiences; bumbledraven read Herbert as defining humanity through open-ended, indeterminate evolution, both personal and species-wide.
