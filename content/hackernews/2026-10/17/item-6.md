## HN discussion

Based on 2 selected comments and replies.

Commenters respond to the headline figure with questions rather than conclusions. One asks how the biomass of the declining populations compares with that of the increasing ones, what ecological roles each group plays, whether the declining species tend to be generalists or specialists, and how large a part human activity plays in these changes. A reply notes that answering those questions could fill dozens of theses and cannot be settled in a comment thread, leaving the reported 73% average decline without the context needed to interpret it.
