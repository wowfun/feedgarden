A single commenter recalls a comparable class project: during a Digital Design II course, the team had to drive a VGA display without using a framebuffer, which the commenter says would have been too easy. They instead built a logic circuit to show a chessboard and a piece as a bitmap, then moved the piece using inputs. An ARM processor on the Cyclone FPGA ran Linux, where a C program sent board positions to that chessboard logic circuit. The commenter describes it as hard and a bit of a struggle, but says it worked in the end.
