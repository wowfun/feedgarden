## HN discussion

Based on 9 selected comments and replies.

Commenters split over the closed release. drum55 calls Voxtral 3 a closed model and notes that TTS and STT remain weak for local deployments. Replying to him, dr&#95;hooo regrets that it is not open like the previous version and recommends Qwen ASR and QwenTTS as good and not too large, plus kyutai-labs' pocket-tts, which supports only a few languages. kalabrium hopes for smaller models deployable locally so speech data is not sent outside, while satvikpendem says closedness rules it out for him immediately, arguing several closed STT/TTS models already perform better than any open one. Replying to satvikpendem, dymk says the real differentiator is not transcription or synthesis quality but integrating a real-time reasoning model over the raw conversation text while hiding tool-call latency. arjie, by contrast, reports good transcription and diarization, the main reason he turns podcast audio into text, and says earlier versions did not hallucinate text when there was no speech.
