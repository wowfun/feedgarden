## HN discussion

Based on 5 selected comments and replies.

A non-AI engineer asks whether TorchTPU is Google's answer to DeepSeek open-sourcing an inferencing engine. Another commenter rejects that framing, arguing large companies like Google do not react to news like that and that the release was likely scheduled for weeks, for Tuesday, and merely coincided with other announcements. A third points out that Amazon is doing the equivalent for AWS Trainium, linking the aws-neuron torch-neuronx project.

On the question of whether TPUs exist outside Google Cloud, a commenter asks directly and the reply says yes: Google has long sold TPUs on premises to large customers such as Anthropic, and Crux AI is now a venture formed to do exactly that. Taken together, the sampled commenters treat the release as part of a broader multi-vendor push to expose proprietary accelerators through PyTorch rather than as a reaction to a rival's announcement.
