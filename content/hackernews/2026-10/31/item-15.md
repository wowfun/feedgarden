## Article

The story begins with a garbage collector in Taytay, Philippines, discovering a woman's body wrapped in a blanket in a trash pile on February 13, 2012. The discovery, initially treated as a local crime scene, became the starting point for an investigative reporter tracing a much larger web of activity. Through legal documents and scattered reports, the trail led to Paul Le Roux, a South African described as a prolific international criminal allegedly involved in weapons shipments, gold smuggling, and online prescription-drug sales. The investigation also notes reports that Le Roux was captured by the U.S. Drug Enforcement Administration in 2012 and later disappeared to work as a valuable asset, a detail that raised questions about secrecy around his case. Rather than recounting a full trial, the piece follows the reporter's attempts to connect a local homicide to a shadowy figure whose reach extended across jurisdictions, relying on firsthand interviews and fragmented records. The account remains grounded in what the reporter could document, pointing to unresolved connections rather than presenting a complete picture of Le Roux's operations.
