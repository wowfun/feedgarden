## Article

After years of becoming a go-to contractor for NASA and the US military, SpaceX is signaling a shift away from signing large new government launch and in-space contracts outside of certain services. The company has told NASA it plans to stop flying Crew Dragon by or before 2030, suggested Boeing take over astronaut missions, and has reportedly halted commercial Falcon 9 sales for missions after 2028 while raising doubts about future Falcon 9 flights for NASA. It also declined to bid on upcoming US Space Force launch contracts and has informed some lunar lander companies it will not provide launch services for NASA's Commercial Lunar Payload Services program. Despite still fulfilling existing commitments at lower cost and high reliability, the change marks a reversal from the era when SpaceX relied on government contracts to becoming an indispensable provider.
