## Article

Scribd, Inc., parent company of Scribd, Slideshare, Everand and Fable, notified users of a security incident in which an unauthorized third party may have gained access to certain user data in one of its systems. The potentially exposed information includes email addresses, the last four digits of credit cards, country, credit card issuer, expiry date and payment history; the notice states full credit card details were not accessed and that there is no evidence the data has been misused. The company said it is notifying affected users out of an abundance of caution and is sending notices in phases. Users are urged to watch for phishing attempts impersonating Scribd or its brands, and are told Scribd will never request usernames, passwords or other sensitive information by email or text. Suspicious messages should not be clicked; users should type scribd.com, slideshare.net, everand.com or fable.co directly into the browser, call +1-877-361-9887 &#40;Monday–Friday, 11:00 a.m.–11:00 p.m. Central European Time&#41;, or report through Scribd's Help Center. The notice cites National Cyber Security Center guidance on recognizing phishing. Scribd said it has secured the affected system and ended the unauthorized access, notified law enforcement and relevant data protection authorities, and is working with an expert third-party cybersecurity firm to strengthen access controls and monitoring. It apologized and said it will provide further updates if anything changes.
