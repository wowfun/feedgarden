Commenters offered mixed views on working at Google today. Ex-Googlers noted culture depends heavily on directors and can shift with reorgs, while current accounts described morale as low and some teams as "hanging on" after layoffs. Others took a more cynical or satirical tone, joking about perks like pods and buffets or LLM productivity, with some lamenting that despite AI tools, Google hasn't shipped more visibly or fixed bugs faster.
