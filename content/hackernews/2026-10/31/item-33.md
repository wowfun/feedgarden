## HN discussion

Based on 10 selected comments and replies.

Commenters said the core techniques are long-standing: ashleyn described “reality checking” and dream journaling to build dream recall as methods from 2000s lucid-dreaming forums that worked well for them. CharlesW traced a scientific lineage to Stephen LaBerge’s Stanford laboratory work and his 1985 and 1989 books. On risks, Pooge said sleep-paralysis associations put them off training and that they rarely recall dreams; ashleyn replied that sleep paralysis is unpleasant but not dangerous and can be used to enter a dream. sonorous&#95;sub framed lucid dreaming as a way to seek visionary experiences without the psychosis or hospitalization risks of psychedelics. throwaway81523 questioned whether deliberately controlling dreams could interfere with their possible “garbage collection” role for the brain.
