## HN discussion

Based on 10 selected comments and replies.

Commenters largely see Anthropic's path to investment-grade ratings as implausible given current AI lab economics. Several argue these companies burn capital at rates that make debt speculative, likening them to high-risk bets where investors could win big or lose heavily. Others push back on pure comparison to past bubbles, pointing to Amazon's early losses and Pets.com's failure as cautionary examples of how hard it is to pick winners. A recurring theme is strategic optionality: some speculate Anthropic or OpenAI could stay closed and monetize a major breakthrough like a cure for cancer through partnerships, though others doubt this justifies current valuations given open models are closing the gap. Skepticism also extends to IPO mechanics, with commenters noting IPOs often let insiders cash out to retail buyers rather than reflecting fundamentals.
