## Article

This Hacker News thread is the November 2026 edition of the recurring 'Who is hiring?' forum. The opening post lays out submission rules: state the location and mark it REMOTE, REMOTE &#40;US&#41;, or ONSITE; only representatives of the hiring company may post &#40;no recruiters or job boards&#41;; one post per company; explain what the company does if it is not a household name; and post only for roles being actively filled with a commitment to reply to applicants. Readers are told to email only if personally interested, and commenters are asked not to reply to complain. The post links to several third-party search tools for these threads and points readers to the companion 'Who wants to be hired?' thread.
