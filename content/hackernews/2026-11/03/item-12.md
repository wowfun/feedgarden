## Article

Apple announced financial results for its fiscal 2026 fourth quarter, ended September 26, 2026. The company posted quarterly revenue of &#36;114.5 billion, up 12 percent year over year, with a company gross margin of 47.9 percent that included a favorable impact of approximately 1 percentage point from tariff refunds. Diluted earnings per share was &#36;2.10, up 14 percent year over year, including a favorable impact of &#36;0.05 from tariff refunds. The quarter set September-quarter records for total company revenue, iPhone revenue, and earnings per share, while Services revenue reached a new all-time high. CEO John Ternus said the results capped Apple's best fiscal year ever and that the company is entering the holiday season with its most ambitious lineup yet. That lineup includes the iPhone Duo, described as the most transformational iPhone since the original; iPhone 18 Pro and iPhone 18 Pro Max, described as the most powerful iPhones yet; a new generation of hardware for the home; Touch brought to Mac for the first time; and the all-new Siri AI. The release attributes part of the margin and earnings improvement to tariff refunds and references consolidated financial statements.
