Based on 10 selected comments and replies.

Commenters noted that OpenAI's post joins a series of recent articles on scaling Git &#40;Cursor, GitHub, Anthropic, and others&#41;, and questioned how much cross-pollination there is between them; one replied that Meta and Google have used similar concepts for a decade or two. Others asked what the massive code volume actually does and why it grows exponentially, with one suggesting AI-assisted coding that produces code that appears to work but is poorly architected could drive such growth. The post drew praise for its plain, hype-free engineering voice with numbers and charts. Some asked for missing benchmarks such as pushes per second. One commenter highlighted OpenAI's contributions to the packfile-uri protocol as a good use of open source. Another was impressed that Pando was built with Codex in ten weeks, with its core designed by a single engineer and implemented by agent teams.
