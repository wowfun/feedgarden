## Article

Hacker Times is a New York Times–inspired re-skin of Hacker News: an hourly-updating site that rewrites HN headlines and adds a short editorial summary paragraph for each link, presenting the feed in a newspaper-style layout. Commenters note the paywall does not appear to be working. Featured stories include: - PHP2Shell and AI-assisted bug discovery: an IEEE Spectrum report describes a California researcher who used AI tools to demonstrate exploitation of a previously reported PHP memory bug; four engineers then spent two months developing a patch. Experts warn faster discovery can burden open-source maintainers, while stressing the work still required specialist knowledge. - MindStudio reports a sharp Linux slowdown on an MSI Prestige RTX Spark laptop: 13.9 tokens per second on GPT-OSS-120B under native Linux versus 65.4 on Windows and 65.1 through WSL, attributed to missing GPU power-management support on Linux. - Kitty 0.50 adds terminal windows that can dock to the edge of another window or tab, plus optional session restoration after logout or restart, preserving windows, tabs and scrollback; on macOS, saved state is encrypted using a key stored in the keychain. - An exploration of early Unix memory allocation, from linear allocators to malloc and mfree functions taking an explicit resource map, including rollback of temporary allocations and size-computation macros in later editions. - Jelly Sheet &#40;summary unavailable&#41;. A “What People Are Talking About” section rounds up several HN threads: direct edits to neural-network weights; a flying Tesla Roadster built with large fans and rocket engines; a Binance hack of about &#36;5.6 billion with roughly &#36;2.4 billion frozen by Circle and Tether; Anthropic’s S-1 and demand/datacenter matching; an Ubuntu user criticizing delay in a kernel security patch; and Karpathy building a video library from HN recommendations. Additional items cover Jessica Hullman’s argument that AI science needs accountable human judgment, and an HN author questioning the privacy of paid Gemini chat history. The page also notes Kitty 0.50’s shader support for triggering on the terminal bell character.

## HN discussion

Based on 10 selected comments and replies.

Commenters compared Hacker Times to several earlier HN-newspaper projects, and one commenter built an aggregator of such aggregators. Others discussed the “Manufacturing Consent” masthead font, with a reply judging the opening copy to feel like LLM output. A recurring theme was value: one commenter questioned why a full hourly site is needed instead of a browser extension, while another said the LLM-rewritten headlines and summary paragraphs already provide a clearer overview than cryptic HN titles. Several noted the paywall does not work. Others expressed preference for the opposite transformation or for HN's timeless original UI.
