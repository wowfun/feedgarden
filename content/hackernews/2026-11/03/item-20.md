## HN discussion

Based on 10 selected comments and replies.

Commenters discussed whether Revolut's self-hosted GLM setup pays off and who benefits. gosen estimated 56 Nvidia B200s at a blended 30 cents per million tokens, about a tenth of prior Anthropic/OpenAI spend, with &#36;2.5–3M in hardware plus operating costs, and questioned the payback period. jacquesm argued self-hosting removes trust concerns about inference suppliers handling data. redmalang said roughly 90% of their work now goes to open-source models, which follow instructions well with precise prompts, though they struggle with ambiguous requests. littlecranky67 described junior developers afraid to experiment because of token rationing. user43928 countered that replacing a &#36;500 Opus budget with unlimited GLM would be a downgrade. world2vec noted their teams still keep discretionary access to Claude Code while using self-hosted GLM-5.3 for most tasks. andy99 called the move a pragmatic form of 'sovereign' AI.
