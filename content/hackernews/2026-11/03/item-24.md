## Article

The poster says their company disables data sharing in coding assistants to avoid leaking IP, but yesterday they and coworkers noticed Codex's “Improve the model for everyone” setting had been silently re-enabled; none knowingly opted back in. They link to chatgpt.com/settings/data-controls to check the setting and to privacy.openai.com/policies?modal=take-control as another place to turn off data sharing, and note the issue was previously reported on Hacker News. They ask whether this is isolated or widespread.

## HN discussion

Based on 10 selected comments and replies.

Several commenters report similar experiences: chriskanan says their Codex training toggle was re-enabled despite two prior disable requests, and kevcampb says Claude appeared to trigger /feedback automatically even with org settings off, with Anthropic unresponsive to follow-up. cute&#95;boi argues OpenAI likely already retains the data, making toggles unreliable. jamienk points to Gemini's “Gemini Apps Activity” setting, which ties sidebar chat history to model training, calling it a dark pattern; lacoolj agrees. monster&#95;truck suggests companies wanting confidentiality should run their own inference, but lukeschlather counters that cost and scale make that impractical. xpct says frontier labs' reputations are damaged and that open models catching up could cost them customers.
