## HN discussion

Based on 10 selected comments and replies.

Commenters offered several readings of the story. djoldman cited Google's blog post framing the change as opt-in: Gemini, Notebook, Gemini in Classroom and Search would be off by default for K-12 student accounts until administrators explicitly turn them on. 6thbit pushed back that the blog does not explicitly say search can only be re-enabled alongside all Gemini features, which the NYT report implies. karmickoala complained that Gmail likewise bundles generative AI with older machine-learning features such as smart inbox, so opting out of generative AI also disables traditional ML. dwroberts replied that disabling those features actually improved their inbox by revealing unread subscriptions. josh&#95;p pointed to Kagi as an alternative search provider available to libraries. Shorel suggested DuckDuckGo could gain traffic as Google search quality declines.
