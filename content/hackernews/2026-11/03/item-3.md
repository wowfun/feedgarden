## Article

Explore central Warsaw rendered as voxels, slightly inspired by Minecraft and MagicaVoxel. You can change the date, time of day and weather, explore the city, or click on a pedestrian to control them, with a touch of Dungeon Keeper inspiration. It works best on desktop but is usable on mobile; the initial load can take a while. The map is built from several open datasets: OpenStreetMap supplies buildings, streets, sidewalks and tram tracks; GUGiK/Geoportal LiDAR provides building heights and trees, with aerial imagery supplying colors; and Warsaw's city tree database contributes individual tree species used for seasonal foliage. The project is open source on GitHub and free to reuse.

## HN discussion

Based on 10 selected comments and replies.

Commenters praised the voxel map but reported performance problems: one saw fractional frame rates in Firefox on Linux with older AMD hardware, while another measured roughly 30 fps but about 120 ms frame times on a laptop with an RTX 3080 Ti. A reply suggested enabling WebGL/WebGPU in Firefox, and the author later posted performance changes. Others raised content accuracy concerns, with one user saying parts of the Old Town and Castle Square looked distorted, with wrong colors and too many floors. Feature requests included live public-transit and car-traffic overlays, and a commenter pointed to similar projects such as Streets of Prague.
