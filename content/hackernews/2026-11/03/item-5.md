## Article

Anthropic now values its internal consumption of Claude, priced at the rates it charges customers, above its monthly employee compensation costs excluding stock awards — a milestone disclosed in the company's IPO filing. In April, the retail value of Claude used internally first exceeded monthly personnel expense; by September, internal usage had grown more than 250-fold since January. The company is not spending more cash on AI than on salaries: it values internal consumption at retail prices rather than at internal cost. Even so, the comparison marks a shift in which AI, not human talent, may be the scarcest resource in technology. Anthropic says more than 90% of the code added to its software in September was written by Claude, while revenue has grown far faster than headcount. The author frames AI tokens as a fourth component of employee compensation, alongside salary, bonuses and stock awards. For decades, talented humans were treated as Silicon Valley's most valuable resource; these figures challenge that assumption by showing internal AI use outpacing payroll on a retail basis. They measure opportunity cost at list prices rather than actual expenditure, and stock awards are excluded from the personnel comparison.
