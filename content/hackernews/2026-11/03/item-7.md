## Article

Polyend has published the firmware source code for the original Tracker, Tracker+ and Tracker Mini on GitHub, alongside Tracker Community Edition 1.0, which adds new features and improvements. Owners can install the firmware on any of the three instruments, modify it themselves and share their own versions; a built-in recovery system is meant to keep a faulty build from bricking a unit. Hardware damaged while running custom firmware is not covered by the standard warranty, but out-of-warranty repairs are available. Community Edition 1.0 streams long samples from the SD card &#40;including on the original Tracker&#41;, adds dedicated timing and velocity fields on every step while leaving both step FX slots free, and introduces new Chance conditions. Six effects and generators can be loaded in the Sample Editor, and a plugin SDK supports building, sharing and selling plugins. Tracker+ and Tracker Mini gain two new synths, CHIP and DIRT, plus a synth-engine SDK; all three models receive wavetable envelopes and LFOs, faster project loading and saving, and tighter MIDI clock timing. Custom Tracker themes and fonts can be designed in a browser. CEO Piotr Raczyński frames the release as support for unofficial Tracker mods and a way for owners to build entirely new instruments or change one behavior. The move follows community reverse-engineering of the closed firmware and interest in alternative firmwares.
