A recurring community megathread collects reports about Claude's current performance, running under the r/ClaudeAI "Performance and Bugs Discussion Hub" format. The maintainers say the thread is regularly among the subreddit's highest-traffic posts, and that centralising reports is fairer than hundreds of individual posts that mostly get no visibility. They note they are volunteers, not Anthropic staff, and that the thread is not a support forum; Anthropic has read such threads in the past, and older megathreads contain bugs that were later fixed.

Accepted topics include bugs, degradation and pricing, but explicitly not usage limits. Posters are asked to supply evidence where relevant: prompts and responses, the platform used, the time the issue occurred, and screenshots. The post also points to companion resources: a log and summaries of recent reader problem reports covering usage limits, performance, bugs and accounts; the official Claude status page; Downdetector for faster outage signals; and the subreddit's survival guide wiki. A previous discussion hub is linked for reference.
