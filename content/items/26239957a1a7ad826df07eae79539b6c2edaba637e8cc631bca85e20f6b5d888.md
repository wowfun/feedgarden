A developer who has been using Google's Antigravity IDE says they ended up using what they call Opus 5.5 inside Antigravity more than the Gemini models. They plan to leave Antigravity IDE and adopt Claude, mainly to get better quotas for Claude models, and their work is roughly 90% coding.

They ask three concrete questions: whether the &#36;20 plan includes Opus 5.5; whether the Claude Desktop app offers an IDE; and what the difference is between Claude and Claude Code. Since they do not like command-line interfaces, they intend to use the desktop app and want to know whether it provides everything the CLI offers. They also ask for pros and cons and migration tips from people who moved from Antigravity.
