OpenAI published a case study on Oracle's use of ChatGPT Work and Codex across recruiting, internal business analysis and engineering. OpenAI reports that Oracle has 130,000 active ChatGPT users and more than 95,000 active Codex users.

In recruiting, a tool built on the products takes a job description and uses it to compare roles, benchmark compensation and assess talent pools by location. Oracle says preparation that previously took two to four days now takes roughly 15 to 20 minutes, which the article frames as a 98% reduction in research time. Beyond speed, Oracle points to standardized preparation giving hiring managers more consistent information across cases.

For internal analysis, Oracle Applications Lab maps business objects, relationships and rules into an ontology. Codex uses that structure to convert business questions into SQL and to call internal systems for analysis, reports or applications. In production operations, the same approach gathers incident context and relevant playbooks; Oracle says simple incidents can now be resolved in minutes rather than an hour.

Oracle's leaders emphasize responsible architecture and security, and describe using prototypes to express requirements and working alongside Codex so that generated code stays maintainable. The article states that people remain accountable for the outputs.
