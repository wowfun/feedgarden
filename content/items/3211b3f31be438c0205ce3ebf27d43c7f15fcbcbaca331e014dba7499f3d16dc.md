A Reddit user reports that on October 9, ChatGPT suddenly began showing a message saying it does not know their age and that additional safety restrictions are being applied to the account. The user says they are an adult and tried to verify their age, but the process does not work.

Behavior differs by platform: in the iOS app, tapping "Verify age" immediately returns error 403; in Safari and other browsers, the age settings page returns "Failed to update age verification status. Please try again." The user says this happens on both app and browser and that they cannot reach the actual verification flow at all.

The post asks whether others are seeing the same thing on that day, whether it is a widespread bug on OpenAI's side or specific to their account, whether anyone has fixed it, and whether waiting for OpenAI is the only option. No cause, workaround, or official response is given.
