A developer is having AI models play Cat Goric, a 2D platformer they built in 2021 for js13kGames, and reporting how far each model gets.

The best result so far is 8 of the 14 playable levels cleared in a single continuous run. Two models have reached eight levels: Qwen/Qwen3.8-Flash-Next and Cloudflare/clef. The poster invites readers to name a specific model to try, and says others can attempt to beat the game with a model of their choice.

The project repository, which includes instructions for running the challenge with different models and engines, is offered for contributions, and PRs are welcome. An attached two-minute recording shows Clef clearing eight levels; because the game pauses while the model thinks, the video is time-warped. The post reports the current best score but does not give per-level outcomes, token usage or model configuration details.
