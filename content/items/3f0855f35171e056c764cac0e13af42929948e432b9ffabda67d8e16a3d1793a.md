A short, argumentative post claims that Nvidia's NVFP4 format is the best available low-precision option and challenges readers to disprove it with numbers rather than criticism.

The author's evidence is limited to two Nvidia Hugging Face model pages: an evaluation section for Qwen3.8-27B-NVFP4 presented as showing parity with bf16, and one for Qwen3.8-Flash-Next-NVFP4 presented as showing parity with FP8. The post asserts that NVFP4 is as good as Q8 and dismisses skeptics for lacking data.

It supplies no independent measurements, benchmark methodology, hardware details or context about where 4-bit formats might still lag, and it does not cite results beyond the vendor's own evaluations.
