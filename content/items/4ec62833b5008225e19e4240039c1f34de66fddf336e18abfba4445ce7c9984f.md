A user observes what they see as an asymmetry in community engagement: OpenAI is active on X, described here as Elon Musk's platform, but is not proactive on Reddit, which the poster characterizes as a platform where Sam Altman is an investor.

The poster says there is no equivalent Reddit presence and contrasts this with Anthropic, which they say is heavily present on Reddit, providing AI overviews and doing moderation or shadow moderation. They describe watching OpenAI's limited engagement as difficult, given that they consider themselves a supporter of the company.

The author adds that they are not on X, having an old account they never log into, which makes it hard for them to follow OpenAI's announcements and news. The post offers impressions and characterizations of platform activity rather than verifiable data on either company's official presence or moderation practices.
