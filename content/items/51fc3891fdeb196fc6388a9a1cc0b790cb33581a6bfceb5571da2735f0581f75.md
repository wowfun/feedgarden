The team behind opper built a benchmark in which low-latency AI "decision" models play Pac-Man in real time against bot ghosts. They say the comparison was prompted by a wave of new alternatives in this space, citing OpenAI's recently launched decisions endpoint and Cloudflare's CLEF from the previous week.

The models tested were jev 1.13, kev, clef, clef flash, GPT-6 Luna and Laya. Their low latency is what allows real-time play rather than turn-based prompting. Each model played 100 games, and the results were published as a leaderboard, with the repository open-sourced so anyone can run their own model and join the ranking.

Humans can also play as Pac-Man against the ghosts, which can be a mix of models or all from a single provider such as jev, kev or clef. According to the author, a game costs about 2 cents, all models run through their startup opper, and free credits were added for people who want to try it. The author reports that it is surprisingly difficult to beat jev's high score and invites feedback.
