A paying subscriber requests a setting on claude.ai that lets users choose whether the Enter key sends the message or inserts a line break. Their complaint is that pressing Enter to start a new paragraph frequently sends half-finished prompts, and that Shift+Enter, while functional, goes against decades of muscle memory.

The stated costs of the current behaviour are wasted responses and consumed usage allowance for the user, and, in the poster's estimate, large amounts of processing capacity that could be avoided by preventing premature sends. The requested fix is a toggle in Settings, which the poster notes Slack and Microsoft Teams have offered for years and which already exists in Claude Code, so the company is familiar with the concept.

No evidence, measurements or Anthropic response is included. The poster asks others to upvote, comment and report the issue through in-app feedback channels, arguing that higher report volume raises the chance of prioritisation, and invites users to share their worst accidental-send moments.
