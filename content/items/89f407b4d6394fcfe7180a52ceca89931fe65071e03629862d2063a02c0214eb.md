A product owner at a large company describes the workflow on what is called an "AI-native" development project and asks whether the approach is sound. In their account, business meetings are held between people and are recorded and transcribed; stories are written by AI and then reviewed by humans. Developers assisted by AI write code and pull requests using Claude Code. AI then reviews the change with AI-written tests, and it moves to a test environment where users test before production. Nothing is live yet.

The author compares the setup to team-level vibe coding and raises open concerns about security, technical debt, and obscure limitations that could become critical infrastructure. They note that many senior developers are on the bench, and that one senior dev argued everyone will eventually work this way because they can and executives require it, while predicting the team will later be blamed for building a house of cards. The post ends with questions: is this cool, are we seeing it everywhere, and do we even know yet? It offers no measurements or verified outcomes.
