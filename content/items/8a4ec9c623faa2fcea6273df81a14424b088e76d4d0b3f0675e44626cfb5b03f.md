A founder recounts a frustrating sequence after the Claude Startups program expansion. Their company, Localia Tech SpA, was reviewed and marked "Verified" on the dashboard from day one, but the mandatory "Claim" buttons for the &#36;1,000 API credits and the Team Plan were missing because of what they call a well-known frontend glitch. They immediately filed a technical support ticket.

The timeline they give:
- Oct 8, 8:27 PM: the official "You're in. Welcome to Claude Startups" email arrived, telling them to redeem the offers. They clicked immediately, but the dashboard was still broken.
- Oct 9, 1:51 AM, barely five hours later: a mass "Update" email said the program had reached capacity and that previously approved accounts were being re-reviewed and potentially removed.

The author argues it is unfair to lose granted benefits only because a UI glitch prevented claiming them while the approval was active. They say they escalated by email to support but are now locked out, and ask whether anyone with the same pre-existing UI bug condition obtained a manual override or a response from Anthropic staff.
