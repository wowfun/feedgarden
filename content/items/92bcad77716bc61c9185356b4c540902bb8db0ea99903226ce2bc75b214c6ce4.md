BrickBench is a benchmark for agentic, text-conditioned LEGO-set design. Given a prompt, an agent must produce an assembly that satisfies semantic and design criteria and can also be physically built, which requires selecting parts from a discrete library and reasoning jointly about local and global constraints.

The benchmark scores validity, alignment and design across three settings that vary in scale and part availability. The authors also provide BrickAgent, an environment for coding agents to construct, inspect and validate their designs.

Results show that leading agents largely satisfy the verifiable physical and semantic requirements but fall short of human designs. The benchmark and environment are released at http&#58;//www.brickben.ch.
