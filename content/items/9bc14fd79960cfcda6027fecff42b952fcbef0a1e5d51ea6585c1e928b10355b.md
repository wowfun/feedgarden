A user asks how others explain the different Claude models to people who are new to the app or to Claude Code, prompted by a relative who is burning through usage on the reasoning that the smartest model is all he needs.

The analogy offered is an education ladder: Sonnet as undergraduate, Opus as graduate, and Fable as doctorate. The accompanying point is that any of the models can do math, but there is no reason to ask the most capable one to balance a checkbook, implying the cheaper or faster tiers are the sensible default for routine work while the strongest model is reserved for tasks that need it.

The post presents this as a deliberately high-level explanation and asks whether it is off base and what examples others use. No benchmark numbers or pricing details are given.
