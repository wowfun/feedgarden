After a desktop update that merged cowork and chat into one surface, a user reports that sessions can no longer be archived. The only remaining options are deleting a session or moving it out of a project, and the user considers both unsatisfactory: deleting loses data, while moving a session out of the project leaves it sitting in the sidebar.

Archiving was the middle ground the user relied on, and they say there is now no consistent way to clean up sidebar history without a compromise. They ask what the reasoning behind the change was and whether they are missing an alternative.
