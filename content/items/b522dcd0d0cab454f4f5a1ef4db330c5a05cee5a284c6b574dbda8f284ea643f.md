A user complains that their usage limits do not reset on the same schedule when they expect them to, pointing to a screenshot of the reset timers as evidence. Their specific grievance is losing 40 minutes of a week because a previous session had hit a limit, which they say does not make much sense.

They argue it would be more user friendly if the weekly limit and the session limit were synchronized. They also report that the session limit stopped them from using the last 10 percent of the weekly limit, meaning the two meters interact in a way that leaves paid capacity unusable.

For comparison, they describe the approach taken by another provider as making more sense: a single meter for the usage you paid for, with no session limits blocking periods of hard work or full utilization. The post is a user-experience complaint with a screenshot, not an official policy statement.
