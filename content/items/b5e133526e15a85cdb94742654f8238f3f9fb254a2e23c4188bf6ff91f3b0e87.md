A user says they were accepted into the Claude Startups program, claimed the credit offer, and were told the credits would appear in their account within 1-2 days.

After some time, the cards related to the offer disappeared from their dashboard. In the morning they received an email telling them they were in and to redeem the offer. They redeemed it, but still saw no benefits in the dashboard. Shortly afterward they received another email, whose contents are shown in the attached image but not described in the post text. The account is first-person with no official explanation provided.
