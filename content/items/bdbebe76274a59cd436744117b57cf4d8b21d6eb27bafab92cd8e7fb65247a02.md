A Hacker News commenter asks whether anyone is working on a standard for positive verification of text origin, meaning a way for an author to actively prove that they wrote a given piece of text.

The submitter argues that distinguishing AI-generated content from human-generated content gets harder every day. In their view this will never really work on the side of the labs, but people who want to demonstrate their own authorship have an incentive to voluntarily collect and present evidence proving it.

The post is a request for pointers to existing efforts rather than a report of results, and it does not name any specific standards body, product or technique. The open question posed is simply: is anyone working on this?
