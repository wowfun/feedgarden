A user who cancelled their subscription because they plan to take a couple of months away from screens reports that the models now feel somewhat “dumber,” and asks whether others have had the same experience or whether it is their imagination.

In an edit, the poster clarifies that they do not mean the models are generally worse. Their suspicion is narrower: that inference or compute may be down-regulated for people who are about to stop being customers, so that a soon-to-expire subscription does not receive the same access as it previously did — what they describe as a hidden extra tier.

The post provides no measurements, comparisons or account details to support the claim, and frames it explicitly as a suspicion rather than a demonstrated effect.
