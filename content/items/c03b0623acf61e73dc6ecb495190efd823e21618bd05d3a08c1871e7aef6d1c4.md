A fairly new Claude user of about two weeks, who is building projects, describes noticing a pop-up under the text entry bar labeled "Upgrade Project" three days earlier and clicking it. After the upgrade, the structure changed: there is now a chat dedicated to managing a project, and the individual chats are called threads.

They say they preferred the previous arrangement. The one improvement they note is that Claude now appears to work in the background on larger tasks while they keep asking questions and get answers within the same thread. They also report that in upgraded projects they apparently can no longer have Claude use the browser as it did before. They ask how other users work with Claude and how they handle these issues.
