A Reddit user shares a workaround for people who are unsure which model is answering their ChatGPT prompts: if the model is not shown in the model picker, go to Settings &gt; General &gt; Intelligence and pick it there.

The poster says OpenAI has routed users to a chosen model before, but complains that this now happens even inside an active chat. They add that they dislike OpenAI selecting a model on their behalf while they are on the Pro plan.
