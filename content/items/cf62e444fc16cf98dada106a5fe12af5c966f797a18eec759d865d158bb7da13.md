A user describes running a two-model setup that pairs Opus 5.5 as the master orchestrator with Astra Max as the worker. They say they are using this combination only because of banked resets and credits received from OpenAI in exchange for downgrading 20x accounts to 10x, and they plan to cancel at the end of the month.

With that subscription ending, the poster is looking for a new worker model to sit under Opus 5.5. They report having tried pairing Astra with GLM and calling the result a joke, because errors meant Astra ended up doing all the work. They suggest Opus together with Deepseek as a possibility.

The stated aim is to maximize usage on a large project they are working on. The post asks for suggestions on which model should serve as the worker in this orchestration arrangement, and includes no benchmark data, cost figures or test results.
