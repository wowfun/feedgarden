A user recounts keeping Claude Pro without paying for about a year after cancelling. They originally bought Pro on their Gmail account, paying through Apple. Signing in with Apple opened a separate Claude account that also had Pro; the Apple login used a private relay address, a random Apple email. While they were paying, both accounts had their own usage limits, so they could switch accounts after hitting the limit on one and effectively get double usage from a single subscription.

They eventually cancelled the subscription on the Gmail account, but the Apple account kept Pro. Claude told them they were subscribed through iOS, yet their Apple subscriptions list showed nothing active for Claude. That state persisted for roughly a year while they kept using the service without paying. They suspected a mix-up because their Apple ID was tied to their Gmail, though the Apple login used a different relay email, so they do not know how the accounts became connected or why cancelling affected only one. The Apple account has now returned to the free tier, which prompted the post; they assume the underlying cause was fixed. They kept quiet about it for the whole year and ask whether others had the same experience.
