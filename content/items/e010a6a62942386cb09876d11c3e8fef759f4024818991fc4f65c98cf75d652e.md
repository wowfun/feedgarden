A developer describes using Claude to build a catalog of emulators, hosted at mockingbird.chrisvouga.dev, with the aim of making end-to-end tests faster. The emulators stand in for popular third-party services. The author claims the same approach could use Claude to build an entire full-stack application that integrates with third-party services and databases and runs in the browser without provisioning any infrastructure, citing a medical-testing example on the same site, and asks for opinions.
