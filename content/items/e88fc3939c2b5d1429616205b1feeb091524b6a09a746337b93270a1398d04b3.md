A user reports a repeated sign-in conflict across Claude apps. They previously used Claude on a work laptop, a Mac and a phone. After getting a second work laptop, logging in on that machine signs them out of all their other devices, and logging in on any other device then signs them out of the new work laptop. The behaviour is described as consistent and looping between the machines, with no cause identified by the user.
