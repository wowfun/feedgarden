Arca is an AI-native company, not a SaaS product, that has raised &#36;64M to build a model-agnostic harness for deploying agents in financial work. Rather than replacing human advisors, the system is designed so that AI handles work around advisors while clients do not interface with agents directly. Human advisors remain backed by always-on agents that cover services such as advice, planning, tax, insurance, and estate work across varied client needs.

The platform routes tasks by complexity tiers without exposing that routing to advisors, and it uses a mix of models with Claude as the primary choice after testing Gemini, OpenAI, and Luna. For long-horizon work, tasks can span days or weeks; when they resume, signals attach to threads or new agents with per-client memory and a "dreaming" reflection mechanism. Human-in-the-loop controls apply to money movement, liquidation, and direct client communications, while analysis is more heavily automated. Safety measures include guardrails, per-write LLM verification, prompt injection detection, consensus between models and smaller models combined with deterministic PII checks, logged actions, sandboxes for compute, and deterministic plus model-based checks on financial PDFs. A component called Jev enforces rigor and prohibits autonomous trading. Feedback edits require a stated reason and feed into evals or meta-agent suggestions for human review, with a noted wariness of fully managed agents and no stated focus on automated trade recovery.

Arca's Orka system maintains a knowledge base of files curated by experts, with a first-class "Knowledge" tab so that agents cite sources and create a traceable feedback loop. As an example, a prospective client with an unresponsive &#36;1500 advisor was found to have hidden fees and a non-fiduciary status. The system is also described as proactive on deadlines such as required minimum distributions. These mechanisms illustrate how long-running, stateful agents can operate in a regulated financial setting under explicit constraints on autonomy.
