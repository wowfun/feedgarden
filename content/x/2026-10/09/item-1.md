In day four of a running update series, OpenAI's Thibault Sottiaux says steering has been improved to be instant. The stated effect is that the model reacts much faster to the adjustments a user makes, so direction can be course-corrected in realtime and the model does not waste effort.

Alongside this he announces the release of GPT-6.1 Sol ultrafast. He says the two changes work very well together, presenting faster responsiveness and the new model variant as complementary. No benchmark numbers, latency figures or availability details are given in the post.
