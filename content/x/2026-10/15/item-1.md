Aditya Agarwal, writing about his work at South Park Commons &#40;@spc&#41;, describes an attempt to reconcile two observations. First, he says he has never seen technological progress this rapid or fundamental, and that nothing seems impossible. Second, capital is flowing to consensus teams, in consensus markets, and on consensus terms.

To address the gap between those two, he describes a new experiment: &#36;200,000, uncapped, offered to a handful of what he calls unapologetically maximalist thinkers who want to pursue a truly out-there idea.

The framing positions the funding as a response to a funding market he characterizes as converging on safe, agreed-upon bets, set against a period of unusually fast technical change.
