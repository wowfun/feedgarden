Aditya Agarwal argues for rewriting the capital stack. In his view, a company building nuclear technology probably should not raise the same kind of seed round as an AI application, because one size does not fit all.

Instead, he says more people need to be working on ideas that seem impossible. He frames the &#36;200,000 as "no-downside fuel" that lets a team explore a hard problem, with the aim of helping them accelerate toward what he calls the right shape of downstream capital afterward.

The post does not specify the terms, ownership, or which sectors the funding targets beyond offering nuclear as a comparison point.
