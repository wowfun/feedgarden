Aditya Agarwal says he does not know what ideas to ask for. Rather than specify categories, the invitation is for people to bring what they consider the most daring and risky, with the stated intent of actively rethinking the organization's prior assumptions.

What he says he does know is that ambition is infectious. The pitch is that founders would come to South Park Commons and build alongside technologists exploring frontiers of their own.

The post directs people to an interest form to express interest; it does not list selection criteria, deadlines, or a number of accepted teams beyond the handful referred to earlier in the thread.
