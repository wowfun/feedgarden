The author argues that simply telling people it is good to "be technical" is not accurate enough. In their view, being technical means being in the details with the right parts of the code, rather than just having technical knowledge in general. For people who are non-technical, being in the details instead means knowing how things work at the right level. The post contrasts these two forms of engagement rather than recommending one over the other.
