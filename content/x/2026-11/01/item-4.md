The author argues that a good test of AI usage is whether your output could have been produced before AI. Until recently, AI mainly sped up work you could already do. Now, he says, AI-assisted output should dramatically exceed prior capability in a fraction of the time: prototypes should be fully functional and feature-rich, architectures should achieve otherwise unreachable performance, research should cover every fact across all dimensions, and business processes should operate differently and learn from past failures. He notes intelligence remains jagged but is superior on enough dimensions that combining it with human strengths yields qualitatively different outcomes. Conclusion: if you could have done the work before AI, you are probably not pushing it enough.
