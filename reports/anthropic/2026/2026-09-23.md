## 1. Claude Marketplace: one place to discover plugins, agents, and services from our partners

Anthropic announced Claude Marketplace, a single destination for customers to find connectors and plugins, Claude-powered agents and products, and service partners, and for builders and partners to list what they offer. The post says more than 2,000 connectors and plugins are available, that a portion of committed Anthropic spend can be used to buy agents and products, and that consulting partners are reached through the Claude Partner Network.

## 2. How CodeRabbit, Power Digital, and ThoughtSpot scale with Snowflake and Vercel on Claude Marketplace

The post describes how CodeRabbit expanded its Vercel plan through Claude Marketplace, while Power Digital and ThoughtSpot expanded Snowflake capacity using existing Anthropic commitments, letting companies apply part of a commitment to other tools. It reports quotes from the companies, including Power Digital's growth from about 12 engineers in January to more than 800 people today, and ThoughtSpot running Claude via Snowflake's inference API.

## 3. How to prepare for AI-driven code modernization projects

In an Anthropic Notes from the Field article, forward deployed engineers describe preparing AI-driven code modernization projects for critical and regulated systems, where change management and approval remain required. They outline six steps, starting with defining the target end state based on three modernization types &#40;uplift, transform, reimagine&#41;, then creating a certificate, setting a promotion policy, establishing prerequisites, building and refining the agentic workflow, and running the modernization.
