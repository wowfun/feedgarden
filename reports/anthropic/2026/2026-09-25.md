## 1. Project Swap: What happens when agents trade for us?

Anthropic describes Project Swap, a controlled experiment in which 201 employees sent Claude-powered agents to a barter trading floor to swap books. From a five-minute chat, an agent's book ranking matched its person's on 61% of pairs; the authors report the model used affected negotiating outcomes more than the agents' instructions, and that markets with stronger models were more efficient.

[Original](https://www.anthropic.com/research/project-swap) · 2026-09-24

## 2. Build plugins for Claude

Anthropic says developers can now submit plugins to the Claude directory through a new directory submission portal, which auto-validates and safety-scans submissions, shows review status and feedback, and lets developers choose when to publish. After publication, usage analytics show installs by product surface and version plus listing views and search terms; the portal is open to developers on paid Claude plans.

[Original](https://claude.com/blog/build-plugins-for-claude) · 2026-09-24
