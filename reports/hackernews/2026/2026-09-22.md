## 8. Show HN: Drop – A rootless Linux sandbox with gVisor support

The author presents Drop, a rootless Linux sandbox for running third-party programs without the main user account, where one compromised dependency could compromise the whole system. Isolation is enforced rather than conventional: each Drop environment gets its own writable, disposable home directory, with selected original config files mounted mostly read-only. It uses Linux namespaces &#40;user, mount, network, PID, IPC, cgroup&#41; and optionally a gVisor user-space kernel. The author notes this is the project's third submission.

## 10. Show HN: JevBench, a reproducible benchmark for typed decision models

The author presents JevBench, a benchmark for Jev-class models that return bounded choices and probabilities instead of text and are claimed to be much faster and cheaper than LLMs while similarly intelligent on the input text. It reports accuracy, latency and price with configurable weighting; a full run is 534 English decisions, and the v1.3 score combines chance-corrected Intelligence, Calibration, Speed and Cost. Reported leaderboard: Jev 74.4, SemIf 73.1, djev 73.0, Winnow-12B Q8 71.2, reflex 4B 70.3. Stated limits include English-only items and a disclosed latency adjustment.

## 14. Show HN: Training a model to identify AI web content from structure alone

Sitefire's Vincent and Jochen describe training a classifier to distinguish AI-generated from human-written web pages using structure rather than wording. Using 2,250 pre-ChatGPT blog posts from 268 B2B sites and AI rewrites by five models, 214 structural questions per post fed a classifier that reportedly reached 98% accuracy on unseen posts, with 77% of AI posts repeating their main point versus 12% of human posts.
