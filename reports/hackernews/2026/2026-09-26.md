## 28. Turning GLM-5.3-Flash into a Jev-like decision model

The author reports an approach to getting Jev-like properties from standard LLMs such as GLM-5.3-Flash by crafting the input prompt so the first output token answers the question, yielding a decision in a single forward pass. They describe the approach in detail for GLM-5.3-Flash and vLLM, and benchmark it against Jev and Laya, finding it on par with Jev in accuracy and speed and substantially better than Laya. They note Jev is still several times better on cost per decision, while their setup supports vision inputs.
